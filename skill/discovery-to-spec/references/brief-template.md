# Product brief template

**Problem.** One or two sentences. Who is hurting, doing what, and what it costs them.

**Goals.** Two to four outcomes the customer would recognize as success.

**Users.** Who will use the solution day to day, and who approves it.

**In scope.** What the first version does.

**Out of scope.** What the first version deliberately does not do (protects the timeline).

**Success metrics.** Measurable, with the baseline from the call when one was given
(for example: "report prep from 6 hours to under 1 hour per client").

**Risks.** Data access, adoption, accuracy, compliance, stakeholder alignment.

**Open questions.** What we still need to ask before building.
