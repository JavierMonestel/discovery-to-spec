Discovery call: Lumen Learning Studio
Date: 2026-09-21 | Duration: 30 min
Attendees: Priya Nair (Head of Content, Lumen), Diego Castillo (Video Producer, Lumen), Javier (PM)

Javier: What does Lumen do and how big is the team?

Priya: We make online video courses for professionals, mostly data and finance skills. We sell to companies that buy seats for their employees. About 70 people, we publish roughly 12 new lessons a week across 25 courses.

Javier: Where does it get hard?

Priya: Knowing what's working. Our video platform gives us drop-off charts per lesson. I know lesson 4 of the Excel course loses 40 percent of viewers in the first two minutes. But nobody has time to watch it and figure out why, and there are hundreds of lessons.

Diego: And the learner comments are a gold mine that nobody reads. We get maybe 300 comments a week across courses. People say things like "the audio drops at minute 3" or "this formula is wrong on the slide". We find out weeks later.

Javier: How do you handle those comments today?

Diego: I skim them on Fridays when I have time. Which is not often. Last month a wrong formula sat in a finance lesson for five weeks. A corporate customer flagged it in their renewal call.

Priya: That renewal is worth about 90 thousand dollars a year. They renewed, but they made a point of it.

Javier: What else takes your team's time?

Diego: Repurposing. Every lesson should become a short clip for LinkedIn, a summary for the course page, and chapter markers. We do it for maybe 20 percent of lessons because it's all manual.

Priya: Marketing keeps asking for more clips. The ones we post do well, they drive demo requests.

Javier: What tools are in play?

Diego: Our video platform, Google Drive for scripts, Notion for the content calendar, and our website is on a headless CMS.

Javier: Who decides on tooling, and what does success look like?

Priya: I do, with our COO. Success is that every week I get a short list of the lessons that need fixing and why, and that learner-reported errors get fixed in days, not weeks. Budget exists but I need to show impact on completion rates by next quarter.

Diego: And please, no tool that needs me to learn a whole new platform. I live in Notion.

Javier: If we did one thing first?

Priya: Triage the learner comments so errors reach the right person fast. That's our biggest risk with corporate customers.
